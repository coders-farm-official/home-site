About Coders Farm

We teach people to code for free, and we build what we teach.

Coders Farm, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Lansing, Michigan. We turn hard working people into employable software engineers at no cost, and we make free, open-source software that keeps your data on your own devices.

Everything we build is open source. Everything we teach is free. We don't collect your data, and we don't sell your attention.

What we teach

Learn

A free curriculum at learn.codersfarm.org: 27 lessons in five tracks, from your first HTML page to a full-stack Java capstone, plus 13 side quests. It runs in your browser, works offline, and needs no account.

What we build

Domovoi

A free, open-source voice assistant that runs on your own hardware. On a phone it answers in airplane mode; at home it puts a voice in every room, and your voice never leaves your network.

About us, in one paragraph

Coders Farm, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) technology-education nonprofit in Lansing, Michigan. Its free curriculum at learn.codersfarm.org takes learners from their first line of HTML to a full-stack Java capstone, entirely in the browser and with no account. Coders Farm also builds Domovoi, a free, open-source voice assistant that runs on a household's own hardware and keeps their voice at home. Learn more at codersfarm.org.
