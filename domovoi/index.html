What Domovoi is

Domovoi turns a computer you already own into your home's own system: your music, movies, audiobooks, files and documents in one place, playing in every room and on your phone.

A server at home

A Windows or Linux computer you own runs it all: understanding speech, the AI, your library and the dashboard.

A voice in every room

A small speaker, a spare laptop or an old phone in each room hears the wake word, answers and plays your music.

An app in your pocket

The Android app reaches your house from your phone, and on its own it plays your phone's music and answers questions, even offline.

Set up Domovoi at home

Put the Domovoi server on a computer at home, then give any room a voice. Everything runs from that one computer, and your voice never leaves your network.

Step 1

The server

The Windows installer sets up the whole Domovoi server on a Windows 11 PC: speech, the language models, voices, your music library and the dashboard.

  • Windows 11 22H2 or later, 64-bit (not Arm), with virtualization turned on in the firmware.
  • 16 GB of memory and 40 GB free, ideally on an SSD. An NVIDIA GPU helps but isn't needed.
  • About 10.5 to 13.5 GB to download during setup, most of it the AI models; about 4.5 GB if you leave out AI chat and questions.
  • The installer doesn't carry a Windows code-signing certificate yet. Turn Smart App Control off, and when Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway.
Download for Windows

Always the latest release; its checksums are on the release page. The full Windows guide.

Prefer Linux? On Ubuntu Server 26.04, one script sets it all up: the Linux guide.

Step 2

The rooms

Any room can have Domovoi: a Raspberry Pi speaker, a spare Linux or Windows laptop, or an old Android phone or tablet in room mode.

Add one from your server's dashboard under Settings → Household → Rooms. It joins with a one-time approval code, and then it hears the wake word, answers, plays music, rings timers and works as an intercom.

  • Keep laptops and phones on a charger, on a hard surface, with a charge limit if they have one.
  • A phone or tablet becomes a room through the Android app, with nothing else to install.
Set up a room

What it does

  • Plays your own music library in every room, and Up next keeps it going.
  • Internet radio, podcasts, audiobooks and videos.
  • Timers, alarms, reminders, shared lists, the news and a household calendar, by voice.
  • Drop in on another room, like an intercom.
  • Knows who's speaking, once each person in the house introduces themselves.
  • Keeps your files, photos and documents in one place, with printing and scanning.
  • Plugins add more, installed from the dashboard.

What it doesn't

  • No cloud account, and nothing to sign up for.
  • No ads, and nothing is sent to Coders Farm.
  • Your voice doesn't leave your network: speech is understood on your own computer.
  • It doesn't decide when to use the internet; your household does. At setup you choose: yes, always; sometimes; or no, keep everything in the house. You can change it later in Settings → Privacy → Internet.

The Android app

With your Domovoi server, the app runs the whole house from your phone. On its own, it plays what's on your phone and runs an assistant right on the phone, even in airplane mode.

Get it on Google Play

Or download the APK from the GitHub releases page: the 64-bit APK fits almost every phone from about 2017 on, and the 32-bit one is for older and budget phones (everything except the on-device assistant).

What you need

  • Android 8.0 or later.
  • For the on-device assistant: a 64-bit phone with 8 GB of memory or more, and about 3 GB of free space.
  • A one-time download of the assistant's models, about 2.6 GB, over Wi-Fi unless you change it.

The phone's assistant can

  • Answer typed or spoken questions with no internet, and read the answer aloud.
  • Set timers and alarms in your Clock app, after asking you first.
  • Play, pause and skip on the phone's own player.
  • Read a document you attach, or a photo on phones fast enough to read one.
  • With your permission: say where you are, start a call or text to a contact for you to send, and read your phone's calendar.

And can't

  • Listen for a wake word. You tap the mic; room mode is a separate feature.
  • Read your texts, call log or notifications.
  • Send a message by itself.
  • Reach the house's rooms, timers or calendar. That's what your home server's chat is for.

Private by design

Your voice and your household's data stay on your own hardware.

Coders Farm collects nothing from the app or the server.

Whether Domovoi uses the internet at all is your household's choice.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

Your house: only to set up. The server downloads its AI models during setup (about 10.5 to 13.5 GB on Windows), then keeps working with no internet at all if you choose “No, keep everything in the house”.

Your phone: once, to download the assistant's models (about 2.6 GB). After that it answers in airplane mode, and your phone's own music and videos play with no connection.

Do I need a Domovoi server?

Not for the phone app: its assistant and your phone's own music, videos and photos work without one. A server on a Windows 11 PC or Ubuntu Linux adds the voice in every room, the shared library and the dashboard. Set one up.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or later. The on-device assistant needs a 64-bit phone with 8 GB of memory or more; older and 32-bit phones get everything else.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet, but one is coming. Get an email when it's out.

Can I run it on a Mac?

Not yet, but it's coming for Apple Silicon Macs. Until then, the server runs on a Windows 11 PC or Ubuntu Linux. Get an email when it's ready.

Is it really free? Who pays for it?

Yes: no ads, no subscription, and no data sold. Coders Farm is a nonprofit, and donations pay for it. Support the work.

Why does Windows or Android warn me when I install it?

Windows: the installer doesn't carry a Windows code-signing certificate yet, so Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, check the file is the DomovoiSetup.exe you downloaded, then Run anyway. Smart App Control has to be off.

Android: Google Play installs it with no warning. An APK from GitHub makes Android ask before installing an app from outside Google Play; that's expected. To be sure a file is ours, compare it with the release's SHA-256 checksums.
