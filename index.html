Meet Domovoi.

A voice assistant for your home that runs on your own hardware. Your voice never leaves your network, and when the internet goes out, Domovoi keeps working.

In Slavic folklore, a domovoi is the household's guardian spirit, often a cat. Ours lives in your network, never someone else's cloud.

  • On your phone: an AI assistant that answers in airplane mode.
  • In every room: Raspberry Pi speakers, or the old laptop and tablet in a drawer.
  • No account. No cloud. No ads.
